# Custom feature icons (v15)

Five assets were generated individually with built-in Imagegen, inspected, then encoded to 256px WebP for delivery. Alpha transparency is preserved. These are decorative icons, not analytical evidence. The city/lens illustration now appears in the hero, delivered as `public/illustrations/telisik-hero-v15.webp` (1200 × 800); its original prompt is in `visual-assets.md`.

The full prompt for each icon is the shared prompt followed by its subject below.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: bespoke TELISIK website feature icon, a single beautifully art-directed optical glass miniature sculpture, square 1024x1024. Saturated cobalt #2457e7 anodized metal and transparent teal #008e78 glass, tiny polished silver edge accents. Consistent isometric 3/4 view, luminous studio lighting, clear bold silhouette that reads at 64px, object fills 76% of square with comfortable padding. Entirely transparent background, no tile, no container, no backdrop, no floor or shadow outside the object. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark. Not a generic flat vector icon, not a stock UI symbol.

## Network

Delivery asset: `public/feature-icons/network.webp`.

Subject: an asymmetric constellation of four large translucent glass pearls of different sizes connected by thick polished cobalt bridges, with a small teal optical ring encircling the main pearl; a custom sculpture about traceable relationships.

## Narrative

Delivery asset: `public/feature-icons/narrative.webp`.

Subject: two thick sculptural folded speech ribbons floating parallel with a tiny sequence of three chunky cobalt token blocks, one final block transformed into translucent teal; a custom sculpture about following wording changes. No DNA helix, no generic chat bubble.

## Coordination

Delivery asset: `public/feature-icons/coordination.webp`.

Subject: three thick translucent optical fins offset on a compact cobalt circular base, each fin has the same set of three physical notches, and a luminous teal thread aligns the notches. A sculptural instrument for examining repeating timing patterns. No clock, no fingerprint, no gear.

## Evidence

Delivery asset: `public/feature-icons/evidence.webp`.

Subject: two substantial transparent glass dossier leaves slightly fanned apart with cobalt solid spine edges, a small beautiful teal optical lens held between them. Few deep geometric grooves suggest document lines, no lettering. A sculpture about cross-checking sources. No checkmark, no shield.

## Impact

Delivery asset: `public/feature-icons/impact.webp`.

Subject: two chunky sculptural cobalt terraces at different heights linked by one thick translucent teal curved optical bridge, a polished glass pearl resting on the upper terrace with a delicate silver measuring pin. A sculpture about comparing outcomes. No arrow, no bar chart, no checkmark, no target symbol.

The analytical visuals remain deterministic UI rendered from source records and explicitly fictional app fixtures. Generated graphics are never used as plotted data or evidence.
